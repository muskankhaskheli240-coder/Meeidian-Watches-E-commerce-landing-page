Why it's built this way

Engineering you can't see, until it matters

precision_manufacturing

In-house movements

Every automatic calibre is designed, machined and regulated inside our own workshop.

We do not rebadge third-party ébauches. Our movement team designs the gear train, escapement geometry and bridge layout from scratch, then regulates every finished unit across five positions before it ships.
verified_user

3-year warranty

International coverage on every movement, honored at any of our partner service centers.

Coverage includes manufacturing defects in the movement, case and dial. Extend to five years by registering your watch within 30 days of delivery, free of charge.
local_shipping

Free global shipping

Fully insured shipping on all orders over $500, with tracked delivery in 3–12 business days.

Every shipment is insured for full retail value and requires a signature on delivery. Import duties are calculated and shown at checkout, never billed separately later.
handyman

Hand-finished cases

Bevelled edges, brushed flanks and polished chamfers, finished by hand under a loupe.

Each case passes through eleven distinct finishing steps, alternating between satin brushing and mirror polishing, entirely by hand rather than tumbled or machine-polished in bulk.
replay

30-day returns

Try it for a month. Unworn watches in original packaging are refunded in full.

We provide a prepaid, insured return label for domestic orders. Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days of us receiving the watch back in original condition.
eco

Responsible materials

Recycled steel cases, vegetable-tanned leather, and FSC-certified packaging.

Our steel cases use a minimum of 70% recycled stock without compromising hardness specifications, and every strap is tanned without chromium salts.
support_agent

Real human support

A dedicated concierge team, not a chatbot, for sizing, servicing and general questions.

Our concierge team is trained on every reference in the catalogue and can advise on strap sizing, movement care and servicing intervals by email or phone.
build_circle

Lifetime servicing

Preferential servicing rates for the life of the watch, at any of our partner ateliers.

A full service, including movement disassembly, cleaning, lubrication and regulation, is available at a fixed preferential rate regardless of how long you have owned the watch.

Our story

A workshop, not a warehouse

Meridian began as a two-person case-making workshop in 1926, supplying finished cases to larger houses that never put their own name on the work.

In 2011, under third-generation owner Elena Marchetti, the workshop began producing watches under its own name for the first time — starting with a single field watch reference and a waiting list longer than the run itself.

Mission. Build watches with genuine mechanical substance at a price that does not require a department-store markup to justify the workshop's overhead.

Vision. To be the reference point for buyers who want to understand exactly what they are paying for, down to the movement's gear train.

Substance over branding

We spend on movement development before marketing.

Repairable by design

Every part is stocked and serviceable for 25+ years.

Radical transparency

Full specs and sourcing published on every product page.
